Start playing

Choose an instrument from the library and tap the keyboard. Sonora starts audio after a tap. Use the octave and keyboard-range controls to move through A0–C8. Select the drum kit to play percussion pads. The instrument library contains 128 General MIDI presets and 47 percussion sounds.

If you do not hear anything, check Sonora's volume, the device output volume, and whether sound is routed to headphones or Bluetooth. Tap a key again after returning to the app. Bluetooth audio can add a noticeable delay; the device speaker or wired audio may be more comfortable for live playing.

Record and export

Tap Record, play, then finish recording. Your performance appears in Recordings, where you can play it back. Sonora records its own instrument output and does not use your microphone.

Choose Audio to export a sound file, or MIDI to export the played note events. MIDI is enabled when the recording contains note events; its sound in another app depends on that app's instruments. On iPhone and iPad, choose a destination in the system share sheet, such as Save to Files. Closing the share sheet without choosing a destination does not save an external copy.

The app stores recordings on your device. Export important recordings before deleting the app or clearing its data. Recordings from the website do not automatically appear in the iOS app, and Sonora does not offer cloud sync or remote recovery.

Language and appearance

Use the language selector for English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish or French. Use the theme control for light, dark or system appearance. These preferences are saved on your device.

Offline use

Instrument sounds and app assets are included with the installed app, so playing and recording do not require an internet connection. Sending an exported file through an online service may require a connection.
