Sonora Music Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12. This policy covers the native iPhone and iPad app. Publisher: seunggeun Shin. Contact: tlstmdrms01@gmail.com.

Sonora Music Studio lets you play virtual instruments and keep recordings on your device. You do not need an account. The app does not include advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not send your recordings, performances or preferences to a server operated by us.

Information on your device

The app stores your language, theme and favorite instruments locally. When you record a performance, it stores the audio, note events, recording name, selected instrument, date and duration on your device so you can play or export the recording later.

Recording captures the instruments you play within Sonora. The app does not use your microphone to capture your voice or surroundings, and does not request access to your camera, contacts or location. Instrument sounds, fonts and the interface are included with the app for offline use.

Exporting and sharing

When you choose to export audio or MIDI, Sonora creates a file and opens the system share options. You choose whether and where to save or share it. An app, cloud-storage provider or recipient you select may process the file under their own terms and privacy policy. Sonora does not automatically upload recordings to us or a sharing service.

Storage and deletion

Recordings and preferences stay in the app's local storage. Delete the app to remove that local app data; offloading an app may preserve it. Keep exported copies of recordings you want to retain. Removing Sonora does not delete copies that you already exported or shared.

Your device's backup or restore features may retain or restore app data according to your system settings and the backup provider's practices. We do not operate a separate cloud backup or recovery service.

Contacting support

If you choose to email tlstmdrms01@gmail.com, the message, your email address and any attachments you choose to send are handled through email services so we can respond to your request. Include only the information needed to explain the issue. Do not send private recordings unless you want to share them for troubleshooting. You may use the same contact to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any necessary recordkeeping obligations.

Apple and other services

Apple may process information related to downloading the app, device backups, or diagnostics under your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Those services are separate from Sonora's local instrument and recording features. Websites or third-party destinations you open are governed by their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For privacy questions, contact seunggeun Shin at tlstmdrms01@gmail.com.
